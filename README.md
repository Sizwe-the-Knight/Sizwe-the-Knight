### FYIN — For You In Need

**Live Public Beta | Launched October 2026**

A free, ad-free food and shelter discovery platform designed to make finding assistance simpler and more accessible.

Launched with 28 sourced listings across Greater Johannesburg, South Africa, and Atlanta, Georgia.

Built with React, TypeScript and Vite, and deployed through Cloudflare Pages.
